Distances from Mumbai and Pune, check-in slots, meals, room types, group bookings and more.

Frequently Asked Questions About Seema's Resort Neral

How far is Seema Resort from Mumbai and Pune?

We are right on the highway at Neral, about 1.5–2 hours by car from both Mumbai and Pune.

Is the resort near Matheran?

Yes. We sit at the foot of Matheran in Neral, a convenient base for the hill station.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Slot 1: 12:00 PM to 10:00 AM next day. Slot 2: 5:00 PM to 3:00 PM next day (AP meal-inclusive plan only).

Are meals included in the room price?

Meals are included only with the AP (meal-inclusive) plan, which covers an unlimited veg and non-veg buffet. Room-only rates do not include meals.

What room types do you offer?

Standard and Elite Rooms, Premium Deluxe and Couple Deluxe Cottages, Bathtub Suites, Family Executive Rooms, Studio Rooms, Quad Rooms and Dormitories. Call or WhatsApp us for the full list.

Is Seema Resort near Karjat?

Yes. Seema's Resort & Restaurant is on the Neral-Karjat Highway near Panchwati in Neral, so it is easy to reach from Karjat as well as Matheran.

Does Seema Resort take group and corporate bookings?

Yes. We host families, friends, corporate teams and larger groups. Call or WhatsApp us with your group size and dates and we will suggest the right rooms and rate.

How far is Seema's Resort from Neral Railway Station and Matheran?

Seema's Resort & Restaurant is about 3 km from Neral Railway Station, roughly 5–10 minutes by auto-rickshaw. Neral is the starting point of the Matheran toy train, so please check the current toy train schedule before you travel. Dasturi Naka, the Matheran entry point where vehicles must stop, is a quick drive from the resort.

Is Seema's Resort couple-friendly and what ID is required?

Yes, Seema's Resort is couple-friendly and offers Couple Deluxe Premium Rooms and Deluxe Suite Couple Cottages. A valid government-issued photo ID is required for all guests at check-in, and couples must carry suitable ID proof.

What outdoor activities and games are available at the resort?

The resort has a swimming pool and kids' pool (open 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM), rain dance with music, cricket, football, badminton, a kids' play area and nature walks. Indoor games include a pool table, carrom, chess and ludo.

Do you have dormitories for large family groups and corporate outings?

Yes. We have spacious dormitories, Family Executive Rooms and quad rooms, plus an AC dining hall for group meals. Customised group rates are available together with the unlimited AP buffet plan. Call or WhatsApp us with your group size and dates.
